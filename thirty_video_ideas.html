Weighted deliberately toward faceless. Your Aug 19 content sync named the real bottleneck: you aren’t recording on-camera content, so the team pivoted to faceless, reusable formats. Twenty-one of these thirty need no face at all — screen recordings, analytics, montages, text-over-footage. The system

01–06

Answers the single most-voiced want on every call: get it done in about two hours a month. Eight of nine agents raised time as the blocker before anything else.

01

Two hours a month, eight videos

“This is a full month of content being filmed. Watch the clock in the corner.”

Film a real batch day start to finish: teleprompter on the tripod, outfit changes between takes, eight scripts back to back. Put a running timer on screen. Nobody believes the two-hour claim until they watch it happen.

How to run this

At two posts a week this is roughly fifteen weeks of content. Don’t shoot it in order — lead with the Proof pillar (07–12), because price is your second-biggest objection and currently your thinnest content coverage. The assets for 07, 10 and 12 already exist in Krystal’s screenshots and the Aug 16–21 report.

Numbers 08, 18 and 20 are the most likely to travel, and 16 and 17 are the most likely to convert someone who has already been burned. Number 16 carries real risk — publishing a flat account — so decide deliberately rather than by default.

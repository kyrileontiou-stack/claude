The audit found the opposite of what you want. Of the thirty videos I wrote, roughly ten are bottom-of-funnel and only three are genuinely top. That’s about 33 / 57 / 10 — near-inverted. It happened because I built the list off your discovery calls, and discovery calls only contain people who are already solution aware. Nobody unaware ever books one, so they never appeared in the data.

What the split actually costs

You don’t need to throw the bottom-of-funnel work away — you need to move most of it off the feed. Seven of those ten videos belong in a sales library, not a posting calendar: the ones your setters send mid-conversation when a prospect stalls on price or asks what they actually get. Your Aug 21 setter meeting already found this — the website video and testimonials were the strongest tools for countering price objections. That is bottom-of-funnel content doing its job outside the feed.

Three stay on the feed (01, 07, 09 below) — enough that someone who has just discovered you can find the offer, the proof and the price without a call.

The real work is top of funnel, where you have three videos and need eighteen. Fifteen new ones are written below. They are about being an agent, not about video marketing — that’s the point. An agent who doesn’t know they have a content problem will not watch content about content.

Two things to decide

The ratio suits a business that sells inbound. Yours doesn’t — yet. You run outbound setters dialling lists, so today most people who see your content arrive already in a conversation. For them the bottom-of-funnel library matters more than reach does. A 60 / 30 / 10 feed is the right call if the goal is to build inbound over the next six to twelve months; it will not move next month’s booked-call number. Both are defensible — just pick knowingly, because they need different patience.

Top of funnel needs a route back. Eighteen videos that never mention your service will grow an audience that doesn’t know what you sell. Every one of them needs a bio link, a pinned comment, or a follow-up post that leads somewhere — otherwise the reach is real and the pipeline isn’t.

Fifteen new videos here are unscripted so far. The three bottom-of-funnel ones already have finished scripts in the format playbook.

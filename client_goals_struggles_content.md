# What Agents Actually Want

Field notes from 11 recorded calls — August 2026.

Every goal and struggle below was said out loud by a real estate agent on a Kyri Media
discovery call this month. Ranked by how many separate agents raised it unprompted.

**Sample:** 9 agent calls (Aug 20–27) — Ben Guglielmi, Nancy Gerrard, Kimberly Rainey,
Kaui Garcia, Maura Young, Tara (TN), Harry Hosseiny, Ira Bethea, Erika Johnson — plus the
Aug 24 weekly report and the Aug 27 retention meeting.

---

## Top 10 goals

1. **Get it done in about two hours a month** (8/9) — Every agent who moved forward did so
   because the time ask was small. Harry wants to "work smart," not add hours. Erika needs
   eight videos batched in one sitting.
2. **Win more listings, not more buyers** (5/9) — Maura wants to double volume and shift from
   coaching income to sales. Harry is listings-first at $650k+.
3. **Move up-market into luxury price points** (5/9) — Kimberly is chasing a $1.7M median in
   West of the Trail. Erika wants Cheat Lake luxury. Tara wants affluent out-of-state buyers.
4. **Prove a return they can point at** (5/9) — Ben named the number: 3–5x or it isn't worth
   $1,500/mo. Ira wants "data over vibes." Brian is threatening to pause because $5k of
   property videos didn't feel like ROI.
5. **Be the recognised local expert** (4/9) — Kaui runs a 28k-member Facebook group and still
   feels invisible. Kimberly wants to own neighbourhood-by-neighbourhood search.
6. **Reach people outside the referral sphere** (4/9) — Kaui, 21 years in, wants "broader reach
   to people who don't know us." Harry runs almost entirely on referrals and knows it caps him.
7. **Look premium, never cheesy** (4/9) — Ben explicitly rejects fast-cut, meme-y content that
   would alienate luxury clients. Ira refuses "vanity" and AI-faced filler.
8. **Own a brand independent of the brokerage** (3/9) — Ben is leaving his team. Tara's
   brokerage site puts the broker's phone number on her leads.
9. **Replace paid portal leads with organic inbound** (2/9) — Tara is escaping Zillow and
   Realtor.com. Erika is cutting Zillow in Morgantown specifically to fund content.
10. **A cadence they can actually keep** (9/9) — Maura will time-block if someone puts it on
    the calendar; Kimberly can't hold multi-platform rhythm "without a structured plan or coach."

## Top 10 struggles

1. **No time to ideate, script, shoot, edit and post** (8/9) — The most repeated sentence on
   these calls. Ben: "not inclined, no editing expertise." Harry understands video's value and
   has never made one.
2. **The price feels unjustifiable against unproven return** (6/9) — Kaui balked at $18k/year.
   Maura called $4,500 "too steep." Harry's current vendor costs less than $4,500 *a year*.
   Tara can do $500–800, not $1,500.
3. **They start, then stop — consistency collapses** (5/9) — Erika has been on hiatus 1–2 years.
   Kimberly films but never publishes.
4. **Everything looks the same — no creative direction** (4/9) — Kaui spends "excessive time"
   hunting for ideas and lands on the same rate-and-process explainers. Brian is leaving over
   repetitive slideshows.
5. **Trapped inside the referral bubble** (4/9) — Strong reputations offline, invisible to
   anyone who hasn't met them.
6. **Burned by a previous marketing vendor** (3/9) — Erika paid $6,000 over six months, was
   pitched $20k/year, and quit. She is now allergic to prepaying.
7. **The editing bottleneck kills the footage** (3/9) — Kimberly owns two cameras, two phones
   and a tripod, and still can't publish.
8. **They hate being on camera** (3/9) — Maura is comfortable speaking publicly but not to a
   lens. Tara wants b-roll and voiceover with minimal face time.
9. **Brand is fragmented or actively leaking leads** (3/9) — Tara's brokerage site routes
   inquiries to her broker. Nancy's draft went out with an outdated headshot and her name
   mispronounced.
10. **The leads that do arrive are the wrong ones** (2/9) — Erika's old vendor produced
    out-of-area tyre-kickers who never converted after showings.

## Ten content topics

For Kyri Media's own pages, aimed at agents.

1. **Two hours a month, eight videos** — *"This agent films a month of content in the time you
   spend on one open house."* Film an actual batch day: teleprompter on a tripod, outfit changes,
   eight scripts back to back. Show the timer.
   → Goal 1 / Struggle 1. Format: over-the-shoulder process.
2. **She paid $6,000 and quit** — *"An agent spent six grand on video marketing, got eight videos
   a month she had to rewrite herself, and walked away."* Erika's story, anonymised, plus the
   three things that made it fail.
   → Struggles 6 & 10. Format: talking-head story.
3. **Your referrals are a ceiling, not a moat** — *"Twenty-one years in business, 28,000 people
   in her Facebook group, and total strangers still can't find her."* Aimed at 15–20 year
   veterans, who have the budget.
   → Goal 6 / Struggle 5. Format: opinion.
4. **The mechanic skit, for agents** — *"If your car breaks down, you don't watch a YouTube video
   and open the bonnet. So why are you editing your own reels?"* Straight port of Doug's 10k+
   format; only the analogy changes.
   → Struggles 2 & 7. Format: skit (proven).
5. **Guess the view count** — *"One of these two videos did 400 views. The other did 10,000.
   Guess which."* Frank's 36-comment question format retargeted at agents. Reveal in a follow-up
   post — two pieces from one idea.
   → Goal 4 / Struggle 4. Format: question post (proven).
6. **$1,500 a month versus one Zillow lead** — *"She was paying $450 a month for leads that only
   wanted land. Here's where that money goes instead."* Portal spend, average commission, closings
   needed to cover a quarter.
   → Goal 9 / Struggle 2. Format: numbers on screen.
7. **You don't have to be on camera** — *"Three agents on my roster have never shown their face.
   Here's what they post instead."* B-roll voiceover, photo walkthroughs with the pinch effect,
   text over footage.
   → Struggle 8 / Goal 7. Format: carousel or montage.
8. **Why every agent's video looks identical** — *"Market update. Just listed. Five tips for
   first-time buyers. You've made this video. So has everyone in your market."* Name the three
   default templates, show the alternative for each.
   → Struggle 4 / Goal 5. Format: opinion teardown.
9. **Eleven leads in seven days** — *"This is a screenshot of one client's inbox after one week.
   He isn't technical and he has no funnel."* Michael's week as a receipt; Krystal already has
   the screenshots for the website.
   → Goal 4 / Struggle 2. Format: screenshot proof.
10. **The three platforms that actually matter** — *"TikTok brings you strangers. Facebook keeps
    your sphere warm. YouTube gets you found on Google. Everything else is optional."* Your
    platform thesis from the Ira call, published.
    → Goals 6 & 5. Format: educational thesis.

---

## Caveats

Ranking is by how many of the nine agents raised each item unprompted, so a low rank means "less
frequently voiced," not "less important" — wrong-audience leads came up only twice but ended one
engagement. This is one month of pipeline, weighted toward agents who took a sales call, so it
under-represents agents already happy with their content.

Topics 4, 5 and 7 also adapt cleanly to client accounts by swapping the audience from agents to
buyers and sellers.

# Funnel Content Plan

The 30 videos sorted by awareness stage and rebalanced to the 10% solution-aware ceiling.

**Target split: 60% top / 30% middle / 10% bottom** — 18 / 9 / 3 videos.

## The audit found the opposite

Of the 30 videos, roughly **10 are bottom-of-funnel and only 3 are genuinely top** — about
33/57/10, near-inverted. Cause: the list was built off discovery calls, and discovery calls only
contain people who are *already* solution aware. Nobody unaware ever books one, so they never
appeared in the data.

Fixing it costs almost nothing, because bottom-of-funnel work doesn't get thrown away — it moves
off the feed and into a sales library.

## Two things to decide

**The ratio suits a business that sells inbound. Yours doesn't — yet.** You run outbound setters
dialling lists, so most people who see your content today arrive already in a conversation. For
them the bottom-of-funnel library matters more than reach. A 60/30/10 feed is right if the goal
is building inbound over the next 6–12 months; it will not move next month's booked-call number.
Both are defensible — pick knowingly, because they need different patience.

**Top of funnel needs a route back.** Eighteen videos that never mention the service will grow an
audience that doesn't know what you sell. Each needs a bio link, pinned comment, or follow-up
post that leads somewhere — otherwise the reach is real and the pipeline isn't.

The 15 new videos are unscripted. The three bottom-of-funnel ones have finished scripts in
`format_playbook.md`.

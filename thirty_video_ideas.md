# Thirty Videos for Kyri Media

Shot list, August 2026. Six pillars, thirty videos, each built on a goal or struggle agents
voiced on the August discovery calls. Hooks are written — the spoken first line is the only
part that decides whether the rest gets watched.

**21 faceless · 9 on camera · ~15 weeks at 2 posts/week**

Weighted toward faceless deliberately: the Aug 19 content sync named the bottleneck as Kyri not
recording on-camera content, and the team pivoted to faceless, reusable formats. The nine
on-camera videos are grouped last so they can be shot in one session.

---

## Pillar 1 — The system (01–06)

Answers the most-voiced want on every call: get it done in ~2 hrs/month. 8 of 9 agents raised
time as the blocker before anything else.

1. **Two hours a month, eight videos** — *"This is a full month of content being filmed. Watch
   the clock in the corner."* Real batch day, running timer on screen. [Faceless / over-the-shoulder]
2. **What eight scripts look like before you shoot** — *"Most agents open the camera with no plan.
   Here's what's on the page before we press record."* Screen-record the script doc, captions
   already written underneath. [Faceless / screen]
3. **The teleprompter setup that costs nothing** — *"You already own everything you need. Phone,
   tripod, free app."* Kills the equipment stall Harry and Kimberly both raised. [Faceless / hands]
4. **Your camera roll is full and your feed is empty** — *"She owns two cameras, two phones and a
   tripod. She hasn't posted in six weeks."* Kimberly's situation. The footage is never the
   problem; assembly is. [Faceless / text on b-roll]
5. **One shoot, thirty posts** — *"This is one recording session. This is everything we got out of
   it."* Editing timeline fanning one session into a month across four platforms. [Faceless / screen]
6. **The thirty-minute call that builds a whole calendar** — *"Once a month we mine your last four
   weeks for fifteen topics."* The topic-mining process pitched to Ira. Sells the strategy layer
   that separates you from an intern. [Faceless / screen]

## Pillar 2 — Proof (07–12)

6 of 9 agents pushed back on price. None argued with a screenshot. Weakest-covered pillar,
highest leverage — shoot these first.

7. **Eleven leads in seven days** — *"This is one client's inbox after a single week. He isn't
   technical and he has no funnel."* Michael. Krystal already has the screenshots. [Faceless]
8. **Guess the view count** — *"One of these did four hundred views. The other did ten thousand."*
   Frank's format, 36 comments on curiosity alone. Reveal in a follow-up post. [Faceless / PROVEN]
9. **$1,500 a month versus one Zillow lead** — *"She was paying $450 a month for leads that only
   ever wanted land."* Portal spend, average commission, closings to cover a quarter. [Faceless]
10. **Ninety-five thousand views in one week** — *"Thirty-five videos. One week. Here's the
    dashboard."* Aug 16–21 roster numbers. Scale answers the intern question. [Faceless]
11. **The metric that isn't views** — *"Ten thousand views and zero calls means you measured the
    wrong thing."* Views → profile visits → DMs → booked calls. [Faceless]
12. **She posted nothing last week and still got found** — *"Zero posts. Eighty profile visits."*
    Laura. The compounding argument, and the counter to portal leads that vanish with the
    invoice. [Faceless]

## Pillar 3 — The burned agent (13–17)

A third of the pipeline has already paid someone and been disappointed.

13. **Why your last video company sent you the wrong leads** — *"Your scripts were written by
    someone who's never been to your town."* Erika's failure mode, diagnosed. [Faceless]
14. **Four questions to ask before you wire anyone money** — *"Ask these four and you'll never
    repeat the six-thousand-dollar mistake."* Useful even to agents who never hire you, which is
    why it travels. [Faceless / carousel]
15. **The $20,000 renewal** — *"Six months in, they asked her for twenty grand. She walked, and
    hasn't posted since."* Ends on the real cost: two years of silence. [Faceless / story]
16. **Everyone posts their wins. Here's an account that stalled.** — *"This client did 2–4k views a
    month. That's not a good number and I'm not going to pretend it is."* Ellen and Leonard,
    anonymised. Riskiest video here, most likely to convert a sceptic. [Faceless]
17. **The client who almost quit** — *"A client told us his videos weren't worth what he was
    paying. He was right."* Brian, and the template library you rebuilt because of it. Before and
    after. [Faceless]

## Pillar 4 — Teardowns (18–23)

Four agents described the sameness problem unprompted. The shareable ones.

18. **Why every agent's video looks identical** — *"Market update. Just listed. Five tips for
    first-time buyers. You've made this video."* Montage the three defaults until it's
    uncomfortable, then show alternatives. [Faceless]
19. **"Just listed" is not a video** — *"Nobody outside your office cares that you listed a
    house."* One listing cut three ways, including the smart-home POV pitched to Kaui. [Faceless]
20. **Rate the hook** — *"Five real agent videos. I'm scoring the first three seconds of each."*
    Rewrite the worst live. Repeatable weekly. [Faceless / series]
21. **Slideshow versus this** — *"Left is the slideshow every agent posts. Right is the same
    photos, three effects later."* The Brian upgrade. Answers the photographer-add-on
    comparison. [Faceless / split screen]
22. **What luxury content gets wrong** — *"Fast cuts and trending audio will cost you the $1.7
    million listing."* Ben's objection as thesis. Five August agents were chasing
    luxury. [Faceless]
23. **Content that wins listings versus content that attracts buyers** — *"If you want sellers,
    stop making buyer content."* Second-most-common goal, and most agents are unknowingly making
    the wrong one. [Faceless]

## Pillar 5 — The reluctant agent (24–27)

Camera fear came up before price for Maura and Tara. Near-uncontested ground.

24. **Three clients who've never shown their face** — *"You don't have to be on camera. Three of my
    clients never are."* Real posts, real numbers. [Faceless]
25. **Four shots you can film without saying a word** — *"Walking up to the door. Keys on the
    counter. Sign going in. Door closing."* Executable this afternoon. [Faceless / shot list]
26. **The one-take myth** — *"That agent you think is a natural? That was take nine."* Outtakes
    stacked before the clean take. [On camera]
27. **What to do with your hands** — *"Everyone's first video problem isn't the script. It's their
    hands."* Short, funny, gets sent to a colleague. [On camera]

## Pillar 6 — Point of view (28–30)

Same setup, same energy — shoot all three in one session.

28. **Your referrals are a ceiling, not a moat** — *"Twenty-one years in business, twenty-eight
    thousand people in her Facebook group, and strangers still can't find her."* Aimed at 15–20
    year veterans, who have budget. [On camera]
29. **The three platforms that actually matter** — *"TikTok brings you strangers. Facebook keeps
    your sphere warm. YouTube gets you found on Google."* The Ira thesis, published. [On camera]
30. **Why we don't do month to month** — *"People ask if they can try one month. No — and here's
    the honest reason."* Converts the price objection from a negotiation into a standard. [On camera]

## How to run this

At 2 posts/week this is ~15 weeks. Don't shoot in order — lead with Proof (07–12), because price
is the second-biggest objection and the thinnest coverage. Assets for 07, 10 and 12 already exist
in Krystal's screenshots and the Aug 16–21 report.

08, 18 and 20 are most likely to travel. 16 and 17 are most likely to convert someone already
burned. 16 carries real risk — publishing a flat account — so decide deliberately.
